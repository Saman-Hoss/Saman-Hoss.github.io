Teaching has been an important part of my academic path. I have taught at different institutes and universities in Iran and the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, working with students from several academic backgrounds. My teaching interests are centered on mathematics, statistics, programming, and quantitative reasoning.

I enjoy teaching because it allows me to present delicate and beautiful mathematical relationships in a clear and logical way. I am especially interested in how young minds analyze abstract and objective topics in mathematics and statistics, and I find it meaningful to participate in that learning process. Much of my appreciation for mathematics comes from my high school mathematics teacher, Mr. Barzegar, who cultivated in me the principles of logic, both in mathematics and in life.
