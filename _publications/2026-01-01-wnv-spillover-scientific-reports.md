excerpt: "A nonacademic summary: 

A warm winter and a moderate spring can increase the severity of the risk of spillover to human populations. This is the main finding of the Eco-Epi model.\\

The idea behind this model is to simulate a “virtual trap” that can generate samples without relying on physical mosquito traps. Instead of collecting mosquitoes in the field, we use a compartmental model to represent mosquito populations and their dynamics.

By sampling from this virtual trap, we construct a Poisson rate surface that depends on temperature and the predicted mosquito abundance (Eco-Epi model for WNV). This allows us to estimate how environmental conditions influence mosquito activity and disease risk.

This model is not site-specific and can be applied in different regions. We tested it across six counties in three states with varying mosquito ecologies and environmental conditions.

Using this approach, the model can predict the long-term risk of spillover to human populations. It also allowed us to identify a relationship between global warming and increased severity of West Nile virus spillover risk."
