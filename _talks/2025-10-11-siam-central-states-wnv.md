20-minute contributed talk presented at the 10th Annual Meeting of the SIAM Central States Section, University of Arkansas, Fayetteville, AR, USA, October 11--12, 2025.

This presentation introduced a probabilistic framework for assessing the risk of West Nile virus spillover to human populations using a compartmental mechanistic model.

The talk focused on how the model can capture mosquito dynamics, environmental effects, and long-term spillover risk while remaining data-parsimonious.

It also discussed how the framework can be used to study the relationship between climate conditions and the severity of spillover risk.
