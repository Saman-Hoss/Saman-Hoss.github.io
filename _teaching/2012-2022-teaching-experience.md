I taught mathematics, statistics, and programming-related courses at several universities and institutes in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq. My teaching experience includes full-time lecturer positions in departments of computer science, accounting, business information technology, and related academic programs.

Teaching positions:

- Lecturer, Department of Computer Science, Cihan University–Erbil, Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq, July 2018–April 2022.
- Lecturer and Quality Assurance Committee Head, Department of Accounting, Cihan University–Erbil, Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq, July 2017–July 2018.
- Lecturer, Department of Business Information Technology, Haibat Sultan Technical Institute, Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq, 2015–July 2017.
- Lecturer, Department of Accounting, Polytechnic University of Erbil, Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq, 2012–2014.
