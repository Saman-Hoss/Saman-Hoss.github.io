My research projects focus on data-parsimonious risk assessment for vector-borne diseases. A central goal of my work is to develop models that are not site-specific and can be adapted across different geographic regions, especially when detailed local surveillance data are limited. My current work focuses primarily on West Nile virus and dengue.
